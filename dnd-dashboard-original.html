💀 Evernight (formerly Neverwinter)

Mood: Oppressive, eerie, cold. Think Mordor meets gothic horror.

Music suggestions: Dark ambient, low drones, occasional distant bells. Darkest Dungeon OST, Bloodborne ambient tracks.

Sensory: Ash taste in air, perpetual twilight, cold that seeps into bones, distant wailing, faint necrotic hum.

🌑 Read Aloud

The portal deposits you into perpetual twilight. Before you stretches a city that might once have been Neverwinter — but twisted, as if reflected in dark water. Buildings lean at wrong angles, their stone blackened. The air tastes of ash and old magic. Undead soldiers patrol openly, their armor bearing Vecna's eye. Citizens scurry past with downcast eyes, and you feel watched from every shadow.

Here and there, you notice something strange — cracks in the air itself, shimmering with warm golden light. Through one, you catch a fleeting glimpse of a sunlit street, flowers in window boxes, children laughing. Then it's gone, and the twilight closes back in.

Concentration

  • Taking damage → Con save, DC = 10 or half damage (whichever is higher)
  • Incapacitated or killed → concentration lost
  • Only one concentration spell at a time
  • Environmental: DM sets DC (usually 10)

Conditions Quick Ref

  • Blinded: Auto-fail sight checks. Attacks have disadvantage; attacks against have advantage.
  • Charmed: Can't attack charmer. Charmer has advantage on social checks.
  • Deafened: Auto-fail hearing checks.
  • Frightened: Disadvantage on checks/attacks while source is in sight. Can't willingly move closer.
  • Grappled: Speed = 0. Ends if grappler incapacitated or effect moves you out of range.
  • Incapacitated: No actions or reactions.
  • Invisible: Heavily obscured. Advantage on attacks; attacks against have disadvantage.
  • Paralyzed: Incapacitated + can't move or speak. Auto-fail Str/Dex saves. Attacks have advantage; melee hits = auto-crit.
  • Poisoned: Disadvantage on attacks and ability checks.
  • Prone: Disadvantage on attacks. Melee within 5ft has advantage; ranged has disadvantage. Stand up = half movement.
  • Restrained: Speed = 0. Disadvantage on attacks & Dex saves. Attacks against have advantage.
  • Stunned: Incapacitated + can't move + speak only falteringly. Auto-fail Str/Dex saves. Attacks have advantage.
  • Unconscious: Incapacitated + prone + unaware. Auto-fail Str/Dex saves. Attacks have advantage; melee within 5ft = auto-crit.

Death Saves

  • DC 10 (no modifiers usually)
  • 3 successes = stabilize
  • 3 failures = death
  • Natural 1 = 2 failures
  • Natural 20 = regain 1 HP, wake up
  • Damage at 0 HP = 1 failure (crit = 2)
