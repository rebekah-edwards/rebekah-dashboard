💀 Waterdeep (Doomed Sword Coast)

Mood: Oppressive, eerie, cold. Think Mordor meets gothic horror.

Music suggestions: Dark ambient, low drones, occasional distant bells. Darkest Dungeon OST, Bloodborne ambient tracks.

Sensory: Ash taste in air, perpetual twilight, cold that seeps into bones, distant wailing, faint necrotic hum.

🌑 Read Aloud

The portal deposits you into perpetual twilight. Before you stretches a city that might once have been Waterdeep — but twisted, as if reflected in dark water. Buildings lean at wrong angles, their stone blackened. The air tastes of ash and old magic. Undead soldiers patrol openly, their armor bearing Vecna's eye. Citizens scurry past with downcast eyes, and you feel watched from every shadow.

Here and there, you notice something strange — cracks in the air itself, shimmering with warm golden light. Through one, you catch a fleeting glimpse of a sunlit street, flowers in window boxes, children laughing. Then it's gone, and the twilight closes back in.

🆕 Crevices of Dusk

  • Planar rifts from the Lady of Pain's return — show glimpses of "old" Waterdeep
  • Citizens confused and frightened by them
  • Cult enforcers actively suppressing knowledge — anyone caught staring gets questioned
  • Some merchants near the Dread Market are starting to remember

🗿 Obelisks

Purpose: Attuning all five obelisks is how the party weakens Vecna’s grip on this timeline. Each site has its own tone, defenders, and attunement challenge.

  • Status: 1 attuned, 4 to go. Kas is also hunting them.
  • Attunement rules: Requires completing the local challenge, then channeling Rod energy for 1 minute uninterrupted.
  • Reminder: Leave the high-level list on the Summary page for table-facing clarity.

1. Icewind Dale — Ythryn

Status: Attuned.

  • Located beneath Ythryn’s inverted tower, surrounded by mythallar rubble.
  • Attunement completed during the Icewind arc. Raven Queen vision confirmed the sequence.
  • Use as precedent for how the arcane lattice should glow when the party succeeds elsewhere.

2. Tovag — Kas’s Abandoned Citadel

Status: Unattuned. Original creation.

  • Domain of Dread bordering Citadel Cavitius; mistwalkers and Kas loyalists patrol.
  • Attunement requires siphoning power from the Sword of Kas’s wards without alerting his priests.
  • Hooks: Jimothy’s Mist Talisman, Kas seeking to overwrite Vecna’s claim.

3. Tomb of Horrors — Stolen Obelisk

Status: Unattuned.

  • The obelisk from Tomb of Annihilation, ripped from Chult and grafted into Acererak’s lair.
  • Environment is lethal puzzle-box design; watch for soul traps and false portals.
  • Adjust lore so the obelisk hums with Realm-wound energy—Vecna wants it for redundancy.

4. The Red Belvedere

Status: Unattuned.

  • Creeping gothic garden from published lore; here it bleeds into Sigil via Crevices of Dusk.
  • NPC lever: a former ally half-consumed by the Belvedere’s roses who can guide the party at a cost.
  • Attunement likely tied to reclaiming the Belvedere’s heartstone before the roses regenerate.

5. Ruins of Karsus’ Enclave

Status: Unattuned. Final lock.

  • Netherese flying city that plummeted when Karsus tried to ascend.
  • The obelisk sits in the enclave’s core amid unstable wild magic, shattered defenses, and vigilant constructs.
  • Attunement requires restoring the protective lattice using fragments of ancient Netherese lore hidden in overlapping planar rifts.
  • Complication: Kas has learned what the obelisks do. He may try to seize this one, potentially alongside Miska the Wolf-Spider.
  • Reward hook: Sword of Kas becomes loot after defeating the vampire form of Kas in this arena.

🏛️ Sigil Sanctum — Allies

🧙 Mordenkainen — Wizard of the Wizards Three

Role: One of the three wizards who cast Wish to stop Vecna's ritual (which instead transported the party to the Sanctum). Researching how to undo Vecna's changes. Discovered how to open the rift that led to the Lady of Pain's maze.

Personality: Stern, demanding, does NOT tolerate foolishness. Has underlying tension with the party (especially Akilles) after the "sticks in victims' rears" incident.

Key info: Believes the Rod of Seven Parts is key. Post Lady of Pain's rescue, has been researching the obelisks. Brought news of the Icewind Dale obelisk. Has lost access to high-level magic in the DFR.

✨ Alustriel Silverhand — Wizard of the Wizards Three

Role: Offers lodging and her unique portal. Former head of the Lady's College in Silverymoon. Marianne's former teacher. One of the Seven Sisters (daughters of Mystra).

Personality: Gracious, wise, genuinely cares about the party. The "good cop" to Mordenkainen's severity.

Husband: Kaelen Shadowstrike — also has memories of "before." Kaelen gave Marianne a letter from "Bronte" urging him to come home.

Key Lore: Was instrumental in defeating Vecna during the events of Die, Vecna, Die! Got the key to the Sigil Sanctum house from that event. Does NOT know about Iuz's role in Vecna's near-takeover of Sigil.

🎭 Tasha the Witch — Wizard of the Wizards Three

Also known as: Iggwilv / Zybilna. Third member of the Wizards Three.

Key Lore: Her son Iuz was the demigod who attempted to kill Vecna with what he thought was the Sword of Kas, which ended up being Vecna's path to Sigil and nearly unmade the universe. She met Mordenkainen in Greyhawk long ago.

Future relevance: The Palace of Heart's Desire (Feywild) chapter involves her domain falling into chaos. She'll discover Iuz unwittingly enabled Vecna's conquest.

Has lost access to high-level magic in the DFR alongside the other wizards.

🗡️ Kaelen Shadowstrike

Role: Alustriel's husband. One of the few people with memories of the world before Vecna's ritual. Part of the Sanctum inner circle.

Recent: Handed Marianne a letter addressed to "My Love?" — from Bronte, urging him to return to his wife and child.

⚖️ Sarusanda Allester — Ulmist Inquisition

Role: Lawful neutral human inquisitor of the tome. Speaks Celestial, Common, Draconic, Elvish. Can cast Speak with Dead at will.

Background: Met the party in Barovia. Her father Galias joined the Priests of Osybus — party killed him in front of her. She knew it was necessary but was grieved.

Status: Traveled to Sigil but asked to be left alone. Connected Olaf with the party. Introduced him at The Gibbous Flagon to Zephyr.

🔒 DM Secrets Index

All DM-only notes live behind lock toggles. Safe to keep open on your DM screen.

🔒 Olaf (Wren) — Vecna Agent
  • Olaf is secretly working for Vecna; his amnesia story is staged.
  • Vecna may want Aldar alive; Olaf’s choices can sabotage or “soften” outcomes.
  • Silverymoon: Olaf’s intel to Vecna helped set the Bronte trap in motion.
  • Post Lady of Pain: Olaf gets flashes of “before” memories in Sigil.

Play pattern: give Olaf moments to choose between party cohesion vs Vecna directives.

🔒 Jimothy — Betrayal Plan (Resolved)
  • Jimothy had been planning to betray the party and return to Vecna’s cult.
  • Ehud’s sacrifice permanently changed him; he’s now genuinely committed.
  • The party does not know the betrayal plan ever existed.
🔒 Bronte Trap — What’s True vs What’s Compulsion
  • Bronte is bound by Geas (Remove Curse/Greater Restoration breaks it).
  • Her anger/grief is real (not purely mind control).
  • Marion is coached; Insight can catch rehearsed reactions.
  • House contains an Antimagic Field device; Vecna Null is the linchpin.

Design goal: emotional devastation, then a hard moral/relationship choice after the fight.

🔒 Aldar — Phylactery Hook
  • Aldar’s throne room is a phylactery chamber, but the phylactery itself is not there.
  • Even if killed: reforms in 1d10 days unless phylactery is destroyed.
🔒 Tasha — Iuz / Die, Vecna, Die! Knowledge
  • Tasha is hiding Iuz’s involvement from Alustriel; reacts defensively when pressed.
  • Olaf charmed/wooed information out of her; this can create future blowback.

  • Rod pieces: party currently has 4/7; Kas is also hunting.
  • Obelisks: party has attuned 1 (Ythryn); remaining visions seen at Well of Silent Conviction.

🗺️ Campaign Timeline

Chapter 1 — Rescuing the Townsfolk

Party met in Neverwinter, recruited by Lord Neverwinter & Akilles (local hero) to rescue 4 citizens captured by Vecna's cult. In Neverdeath Graveyard, disrupted a multiversal reality-rewriting ritual. Couldn't stop it, but the disruption meant they retained memories of "before."

Chapter 1 cont. — The Undoing of the Multiverse

Cast through a Crevice of Dusk to Evernight (Shadowfell reverse of Neverwinter). Visited a market, traversed the Dolindar tomb (Zephyr's long-lost relatives). Returned to Material Plane to find it corrupted — transformed into the Doomed Forgotten Realms. Major discovery: King Aldar, who Akilles killed to prevent his Vecna transformation, still rules the Sword Coast as a lich in this alternate reality. LEVEL UP

Chapter 2 — The Wizards Three

Magically transported to Sigil, specifically the Sigil Sanctum. Met the Wizards Three (Mordenkainen, Alustriel, Tasha) plus Kaelen Shadowstrike — the only others with "before" memories. Their Wish spell transported the party here instead of stopping Vecna's ritual. Enlisted party to find the Rod of Seven Parts (Kas is hunting it too). Learned Akilles is true heir to the Sword Coast throne. Some of Jimothy's Vecna cult past revealed.

Chapter 2 cont. — Web's Edge, Underdark

Retrieved Rod Piece #1 from a spiderdragon in Lolth's domain (Web's Edge). Kas appeared mid-fight — Ehud cut off his left arm, party secured the piece. Kas escaped. The infamous "sticks incident" → Mordenkainen rebukes party. Ongoing tension with Mordenkainen, especially Akilles.

Chapter 3 — Lambent Zenith, Astral Sea (Part 1)

Traveled to the Astral Sea seeking Rod Piece #2. Found the wrecked Spelljammer ship Lambent Zenith in three broken segments lodged in a dead god's corpse (Havock). Investigated the stern segment, discovered Ilren (disguised death slaad) controlling the crew. Defeated Ilren, but he used Plane Shift to cast Akilles to the Cyre 1313 train.

Side Quest — The Cyre 1313 Train (Eberron)

Akilles found himself on the cursed Mourning Rail in Eberron. Took on undead features. Teamed up with Skai Kor (goblin artificer), Creak (warforged barbarian), Valrick (human warlock), and Sorel (gnome cleric). Learned about the Last War, docents, colossi. Met Starrin d'Cannith (engineer-slave conducting the train eternally). Akilles fulfilled a broken contract via Kolyarut Malthadrax, exiting to the Hall of Concordance in Sigil. On his way back to the Sanctum, noticed a disruption in reality — would later become the entrance to the Lady of Pain's maze.

Chapter 3 cont. — Lambent Zenith, Parts 2 & 3

Party navigated the remaining ship segments. Retrieved Rod Piece #2 from a hertilod in the heart of the dead god Havock. LEVEL UP

Chapter 4 — The Ruined Colossus, Eberron

Retrieved Rod Piece #3 from Landro, a colossus on Mount Ironrot. Used a docent to interface with it. Accompanied by Kalyth (Cyran veteran) and Mercy (Warforged). Those two stayed in Eberron to lead their peoples to unity. Triggered Landro's explosion, killed the local antagonist. LEVEL UP
