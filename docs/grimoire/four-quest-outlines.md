# Eve of Ruin — Four New Quest Outlines

*Doomed Forgotten Realms · Party Level 15 · Drafted 6/26/26*

These four beats follow the defeat of King Algar's doppelganger in Castle Waterdeep. **Tonight's likely path:** the Phylactery hunt, then Silverymoon/Bronte. **Feywild** and **Tovag** are the two "any order" chapters that follow. Combat is tuned with the standing assumption that the party is over-equipped and over-leveled, so every encounter layers environmental hazards, magic-suppression, minion waves, and time pressure to keep things dangerous.

---

## 1. The Vault of Ended Kings — Destroying Algar's Phylactery

**Goal:** Find and unmake Algar's phylactery so he cannot reform (he otherwise returns in 1d10 days). A short, time-pressured delve beneath Castle Waterdeep.

**Core idea — "one secret among many":** The phylactery is hidden the way Vecna hides everything — buried in plain sight among hundreds of near-identical secrets. The Vault holds the bonewhite canopic urns of *every* Sword Coast monarch who swore to Vecna across the doomed timeline (deliberately echoing the Bonewhite Monolith aesthetic). The party must crack **Three Seals of Secrecy.**

### Entry
After the throne-room victory, the converted "phylactery chamber" yields a concealed descent (behind/beneath the throne). A stair drops into the Vault of Ended Kings. **Clock:** Algar reforms in 1d10 days, and the cult, sensing his fall, is already moving to relocate the phylactery — so the party is racing both a timer and active opposition.

### Laeren, the Keeper (old loyalist to House Castor)
Before or at the vault doors, the party can find **Laeren** — the last hereditary Keeper of the Vault, who in the "before" secretly served Akilles's family. He is aged, frightened, and visibly shaken by Akilles (who wears the face of the man Laeren has been forced to serve). Torn between loyalty to the Castor line and terror of Vecna's reach, Laeren won't hand over what he knows freely. The party leverages it using their **power-of-secrets** mechanic (or earns his trust). What he gives:
- The phylactery is one of the ended-kings' urns, revealed only by **Castor blood**.
- It is protected by a **soul-anchor** that tethers Algar's soul to the hoarded deaths of all the ended kings.

*Laeren can survive as a recovered ally/NPC, or be lost — DM's call based on play.*

### The Three Seals
1. **Blood of House Castor.** Among the identical urns, only the true one *wakes* when touched by the blood of Algar's bloodline. **Akilles — born Algar Castor, the rightful heir — is the key.** A drop of his blood makes the real urn weep necrotic ichor. The emotional spine of the quest: the man hunts his own doppelganger's soul with the blood they share. (Optional cost: while bleeding for the ritual, Akilles's *Unseen Pursuer* bane intensifies — the watcher feels close in this place.)
2. **The Keeper's Secret.** Blood reveals *which* urn but not how to end it. Laeren's secret names the **soul-anchor** as the true lock.
3. **Sever the Anchor.** Smashing the urn alone fails — the soul-anchor (the **Choir of Ended Kings**, a ring of sarcophagi channeling stored death at the vault's heart) must be severed first. Only then is the urn destructible.

### Combat & difficulty layering
- **Guardians:** a Death Knight "Lord-Regent" (an echo of a former Vecna-king) commanding **Wight Kings / Boneclaws** and waves of skeletal honor-guard **minions**.
- **Magic suppression — "The Silence of Kings":** within the Choir's ring, spells of 6th level and higher have a 50% chance to fizzle (the vault hoards more than the dead — it hoards *power*). Forces the over-leveled party off their strongest tools.
- **Hoarded Death (environmental):** while the soul-anchor takes damage, it pulses necrotic energy each round (area damage, no healing above half in the aura).
- **The urns rise:** disturbing the wrong urns, or the moment the anchor is struck, raises the **lesser ended kings** as a minion horde — escalating swarm pressure.
- **Cult reinforcement (the clock made flesh):** a final wave of **Hands of Vecna + a nothic** arrives to stop the party, racing them to the urn.

### Climax & rewards
Sever the anchor → Akilles (Castor blood) strikes the true urn → the phylactery dies and Algar is ended *permanently*. As it shatters, a last whisper of Vecna (or Algar) brushes the party. Backlash: the hoarded deaths surge and the vault begins to collapse — a short timed escape.
- **Reward:** Algar permanently dead; a **secret learned** (power-of-secrets) — e.g., a thread about how Vecna distributes his phylacteries, or a truth about King Aldar (Akilles's father).
- **Vecna card draw:** this is a major blow to Vecna — trigger a card-draw moment per your mechanic.
- **Beat:** Akilles confronts his own bloodline and identity head-on.

## 2. Silverymoon & the Bronte Trap (Adjusted)

**Goal:** Marianne reunites with his wife Bronte and son Marion — and walks into a Vecna trap. **Adjustment:** reconcile the Campaign Notes (the letter was *genuinely* Bronte's) with the dashboard's **Geas** framing — Bronte's cooperation is compelled, not chosen, and **Remove Curse / Greater Restoration breaks it.** Her grief and resentment underneath, though, are *real*.

### The reconciliation (what's actually true)
In the Doomed Forgotten Realms, the Marianne who married Bronte **died years ago.** Bronte, recruited through grief, was bound under a **Geas** to Vecna's cult. The letter she wrote ("Dear, how I have missed you…") was real — but it became bait the moment **Olaf told Vecna of Marianne's desire to reconnect.** So Bronte *appears* a willing betrayer (the surface of your Notes), while the truth is compulsion (the dashboard). The tragedy: the party can't easily tell which, and even freed, the woman is not the wife Marianne remembers.

### Silverymoon — the hollow city
The city still gleams, but the gleam is hollow: the Moonbridge arcs in unchanged silver light, yet windows are shuttered, the **Lady's College stands dark and abandoned** (where Marianne trained under Alustriel — a quiet gut-punch), and a city once famous for music sits in **silence.** **Lady of Pain ripple effects:** unstable Crevices of Dusk flicker at the edges.

**Key locations:** Bonhomme Smithy (Marianne's father — uninvolved, emotional, can warn about Bronte's changed behavior if asked carefully) · Bronte's Home **(TRAP — antimagic device in the walls)** · Lady's College (dark) · Moonbridge · Silver Chalice Tavern.

### Flow
1. **The warm welcome.** Bronte greets Marianne as though no time passed; Marion (11) is present. Everything seems *perfect.*
2. **Red flags (let players earn the dread):** Bronte never asks how Marianne is alive (Insight DC 16) · Detect Magic reveals the Geas · she stalls with talk of dinner · she touches her pendant (the signal).
3. **The trap springs.** The pendant signal triggers the ambush.

### Combat & difficulty layering
- **Enemies:** the **Vecna Null** (homebrew antimagic threat) + **4 Vecna Cult Assassins.**
- **Antimagic device (anti–over-level mechanic):** the home suppresses the party's magic — forcing the casters to improvise, a real equalizer for an over-powered party.
- **Marion as a complication:** the boy is present — no area-of-effect blasting without risk. Moral pressure throughout.
- **Bronte as a shield:** the assassins use the Geas-bound Bronte defensively; harming her is a wrenching choice.
- **Environmental:** the house is rigged — collapsing beams, alchemical fire — and a **Crevice of Dusk** can vent in reinforcements.

### Resolution
Break the Geas (Remove Curse / Greater Restoration) to free Bronte — but her grief and rage remain ("You LEFT us! You DIED! Vecna gave us PURPOSE when you gave us NOTHING!"). What becomes of Bronte? Of Marion? Does Marianne stay, or take them? **Emotional devastation is the point.**
- **DM secret (table tension):** Olaf (Wren) caused this by informing Vecna. Plant it; don't reveal it.

## 3. The Palace of Heart's Desire (Feywild — 5th Rod Piece)

**Goal:** Claim the 5th Rod fragment (**Find the Path**) from the heart of Zybilna's collapsing Feywild palace. *Replaces "Night of Blue Fire on Krynn."* **Villain-free:** the threat is temptation, entropy, and a memory-restoration puzzle — not a big bad.

### Premise
Rod piece 4's attunement points the portal to the Feywild. The **Palace of Heart's Desire** has fallen into disarray, its shifting halls responding erratically to the emotional turmoil of its inhabitants. **Zybilna's** (Iggwilv / Tasha's) fractured memories have spiraled the palace into chaos, and the fifth Rod fragment is trapped in the heart of the volatile maze. To reach it, the party must help Zybilna regain control by reassembling her lost memories — including her tragic realization that her son **Iuz** unwittingly enabled Vecna's conquest.

### The desire-maze
The palace reshapes to each PC's deepest desire. Each faces a **tailored temptation** — a vision offering the thing they most want, "real" if only they stay:
- **Akilles:** the doomed timeline undone — crowned, brother safe, father redeemed and alive.
- **Olaf:** his true self / Vecna's favor. *Dangerous for his secret* — the palace surfaces "before" flashes (which he's begun experiencing since the Lady of Pain was freed). A live wire for Wren.
- **Jimothy:** his murdered uncle returned — or Ehud back, the betrayal-guilt erased.
- **Marianne:** Bronte and Marion whole and happy — *directly echoes Silverymoon if run after it; devastating.*
- **Zephyr:** freedom from the pronged belt and the Raven Queen's service — or his stolen years and his ancestors' redemption.

### Memory restoration
Stabilizing the palace (and reaching the piece) requires gathering **Zybilna's shattered memory-shards**, scattered through the desire-halls — each typically claimed only by *rejecting* the temptation it's hidden inside. Assembling them tells her tragedy and reveals the **Iuz secret** (his failed strike with a forged Sword of Kas became Vecna's path toward Sigil). **This is huge leverage:** Alustriel does *not* know Iuz's role.

### Difficulty layering (no villain, still deadly)
- **The maze as hazard:** shifting geometry, localized reverse-gravity, **emotion-fueled wild-magic surges**, and illusions that deal *real* damage if believed.
- **Heart's Shadows:** a PC who lingers in a temptation spawns a mirror-duplicate of themselves — fighting versions of their own over-tuned builds is one of the few fair challenges for this party.
- **Emotion-locked doors:** passage requires genuinely feeling/expressing, not rolling.
- **Bliss-trap (failure state):** fail the tests and the palace simply *keeps* you, content — a soft TPK by contentment.

### Climax & rewards
The heart of the maze yields the Rod piece only once the party helps **Zybilna accept the painful Iuz memory** instead of fleeing into desire.
- **Reward:** 5th Rod piece (**Find the Path**); Zybilna restored as an ally (a Wizard of the Three); the **Iuz secret**; possibly Tasha recovering some of her lost magic. **Level up.**

## 4. The Bonewhite Monolith (Tovag — 2nd Obelisk Attunement)

**Goal:** Attune the **Bonewhite Monolith** in Tovag, Kas's abandoned Domain of Dread. Reached via Jimothy's mist talisman. **Kas stays absent** — the whole domain plays as dread of the throne he might reclaim, seeding his reintroduction for the endgame. Original, lore-true design.

### Lore footing
Tovag is **Kas's** Domain of Dread, set near the **Negative Energy Plane**, where the betrayer-lieutenant was exiled and remade as a **vampire**, warring endlessly against **Vecna's adjacent domain of Cavitius** through their armies. Volcanic peaks, blood-hued sky, scorched runes that smolder but never burn — matching the obelisk's clue-vision.

### Entry
Using the **Talisman of Bleeding Veils**, the party walks the mists (or rides a Cyre 1313 *Planar Resonance Window*) and disembarks at Tovag, near **Kas's abandoned basalt citadel** on its crag.

### The empty throne
The domain is frozen in the aftermath of eternal war — battlefields of vampiric spawn and undead legions locked mid-clash, the citadel silent on its height. **Kas is gone** (out hunting the Rod and obelisks himself), and the leaderless domain is restless and dangerous. Build terror *without* him: his throne, his war-records, the sword-shaped absence on the wall, remnants that flinch at the thought of his return — and the dread that attuning the obelisk might *draw his attention.*

### The obelisk
The **Bonewhite Monolith** stands warm to the touch within a stone circle ringed by gray mist and rising red embers, the two volcanic peaks behind it. **Zephyr's hook:** his Dolindar lineage stirs — he sees fuzzy visions of his ancestors' failed struggle to control the Netherese obelisks. The Rod piece reacts; Netherese runes flash across the stone as attunement begins.

### Combat & difficulty layering
- **Domain hazards (anti–over-level):** **necrotic mists** off the Negative Energy Plane — *creatures cannot regain hit points in the mist* and suffer max-HP drain over time; plus ash storms and lava fields across a collapsing battlefield.
- **Leaderless host:** Kas's vampiric spawn and Vecna's undead remnants reignite their war *around* the party — vampire spawn swarms, a **vampire knight-commander** (Kas's lingering lieutenant) as an elite, wraiths, and a necrotic colossus-echo as a terrain threat.
- **Attune-under-fire:** attunement takes several rounds while escalating waves attack — a hold-the-line defense with minions + elites + embers igniting the field.
- **The almost-Kas scare (climax):** as attunement completes, every sign says **Kas is returning** — the mist parts, his coffin stirs, his sword's call hums through the stone, the domain pulses. The party must finish and **flee before he arrives.** Pure dread, no actual Kas.

### Rewards & endgame seed
- 2nd obelisk attuned (**Bonewhite Monolith**); Zephyr's lore revelations deepen.
- A clue/relic pointing toward the **Sword of Kas** (which Zephyr is fated to find later).
- The party leaves *certain Kas is coming* — setting up his confusion-sowing reintroduction in the final battles. **Level up.**
