5. Ruins of Karsus’ Enclave

The final obelisk is located within the ruins of Karsus’ Enclave, a Netherese flying city that plummeted to the earth when Karsus attempted to ascend to godhood. The obelisk lies in the deepest part of the city, surrounded by unstable wild magic zones, shards of ancient enchantments, and Netherese constructs that still guard their domain.

To attune to this obelisk, the party must restore its shattered magical defenses, using fragments of ancient Netherese lore to protect it from Vecna’s influence. These fragments are hidden in overlapping planar rifts caused by the enclave’s crash.

While the party tries to find and attune to the final obelisk, they encounter Kas, who has discovered the function of the obelisks and wanted to attune to them so that he might overtake Vecna’s rule. Depending on how things go, he may have already released and partnered with Miska the Wolf-Spider to confront the party. The party can take the Sword of Kas here after defeating the vampire.
