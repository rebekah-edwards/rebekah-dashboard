🗿 Obelisks

1. Icewind Dale (attuned)

Located at the Ythryn crash site near Hearthvault. The party has already attuned to this obelisk.

2. Tovag, near Kas' abandoned citadel

Entirely homebrew. Located in the Domain of Dread near Kas's citadel or Citadel Cavitius. Potential for Kas encounter or Domain of Dread mechanics.

3. Tomb of Horrors

The obelisk from Tomb of Annihilation, transported here somehow. Adjust from D&D lore to fit the campaign. Possible links to Acererak or Chult elements.

4. The Red Belvedere

From D&D lore, adjusted to fit. Details to be fleshed out — perhaps a tower or structure with red themes, guardians, or puzzles.

5. Ruins of Karsus’ Enclave

A Netherese flying city that plummeted to the earth when Karsus attempted to ascend to godhood. The obelisk lies in the deepest part of the city, surrounded by unstable wild magic zones, shards of ancient enchantments, and Netherese constructs that still guard their domain.

To attune to this obelisk, the party must restore its shattered magical defenses, using fragments of ancient Netherese lore to protect it from Vecna’s influence. These fragments are hidden in overlapping planar rifts caused by the enclave’s crash.

While the party tries to find and attune to the final obelisk, they encounter Kas, who has discovered the function of the obelisks and wanted to attune to them so that he might overtake Vecna’s rule. Depending on how things go, he may have already released and partnered with Miska the Wolf-Spider to confront the party. The party can take the Sword of Kas here after defeating the vampire.
