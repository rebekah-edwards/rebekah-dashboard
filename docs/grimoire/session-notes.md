## Session 26 — 06/26/26 (The Vault, and the Liberation of Waterdeep)

- The party initially **dismissed Laeren** shortly after he let them into the underground vault. Realizing later he had vital information, **Olaf, Mari, and Akilles** went back to question him about how to find and destroy the phylactery. Mari had already broken **one** soul-anchor chain, but didn't damage a second (so no Sentinel was summoned).
- *DM note:* chose **not** to use the antimagic field here — saving that mechanic for the Silverymoon arc so it wouldn't feel cheap.
- *DM note:* party has several members **resistant to necrotic** (sometimes multiple times). Favor enemies that deal **force or lightning** when story-appropriate.
- **Reward:** the boon was given as **5 shared single-use saving-throw successes** for the party (not one), to better fit how overpowered they already are.
- After the vault, the party wanted to secure the townspeople. **Mari sent a Message/Sending to Aldir** (in Hearthvault) that Algar was defeated and leadership was needed. **Aldir asked that Corwin Ashvale** — the Waterdeep Resistance leader who'd been posing as a low-level Cult of Vecna member — **run the city** until Aldir can come down and address the regime change.
- **Jimothy met Laqueesha**, the loud, brash mother of a "child" with Algar. (Algar was a lich and she is human, so what she's actually bearing is a mystery.) He also met a **"butler" of Algar's** — a **tortle in a suit**, actually one of the lower-level wizards Algar kept for routine magic (name never discovered).
- **Olaf, Akilles, and Mari found Ashvale** behind the Temple of Tyr, explained what happened, and asked him to gather the Resistance to take the castle by show of force. He asked **Jennifer** for help; together they gathered the **Waterdeep Resistance** in the Temple of Tyr and approached the castle. With **Jimothy still wearing the Crown of Lies (as Algar)**, the remaining castle guards were ordered to let the people in.
- In the throne room, **Olaf explained** as Jimothy dropped the disguise. The guards, outnumbered and no longer backed by powerful supporters, accepted the change of leadership. A nervous guard named **Hamilton** — later found to be a simple **flautist** — was placed in charge of the remaining guard by Olaf.
- The party spent the rest of the day in Waterdeep before heading to Silverymoon. **Mari sent Sendings to Bronte:**
  - *Mari:* arriving the next morning; asked to meet at a **tavern, without Marion**, to discuss before bringing the boy's "dead dad" back into his life.
  - *Bronte:* "No guarantees. Being a single mother has left me in a complex situation to ensure our son is cared for. Look forward to seeing you."
  - *Mari:* "I have some assistance for you when we meet."
  - *Bronte:* "I would certainly hope so."
- The party **looted the royal coffers: 87,000 gold, split evenly** (~17,400 each), and each found **one magic item** (player's pick, DM-approved).
- **Session ended** as the party transported themselves to **Silverymoon.**

## Session 25 — 04/24/26 (End of the Algar Fight)

- Marianne put **Oil of Slipperiness** on the whole party: unaffected by difficult terrain; spells/effects can't reduce speed or cause paralyzed/restrained; swim speed = speed; spend 5 ft. to auto-escape nonmagical restraints (or escape a restrained-condition grapple).
- Kaelen led them into the market to a gnome's shop. After haggling, Marianne used a **Bag of Holding rip to cast the gnome, Reginald, into the Astral Sea.** His 1-ft-tall son **Pibbin** walked in and spoke with Akilles, recognizing him as the king. Pibbin shared that his mother recently died, their house burned down, and they've been staying in the back of the shop.
- **Zephyr** was furious and demanded Marianne bring Reginald back. **Jimothy** burned two high-level spells to retrieve Reginald. Zephyr threatened to kick Marianne out of the party if he ever did that again; Marianne said his experiment worked.
- They reached Castle Waterdeep (throne room empty). **Jonathan used the Crown of Lies** to look like Algar and told the guards to leave; they headed toward Algar's chambers, and Algar returned with them. *(DM reworked the fight to add a "wraith bodyguard captain.")*
