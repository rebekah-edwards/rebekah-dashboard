# Silverymoon & the Bronte Trap

*Marianne wants to find his wife Bronte and son Marion in Silverymoon. The reunion is a Vecna trap, built so that Bronte almost certainly dies — with Marion present so the party can't just unload their full power. Descriptions here are kept plain; add your own color at the table.*

> **The truth (DM only).** In the Doomed Forgotten Realms, the Marianne who married Bronte **died about 10–11 years ago, when Marion was an infant.** *How:* **Umberlee's bargain came due.** Pelor's deal in the shipwreck near Neverwinter only bought Marianne a *reprieve*, not a pardon — and years later, after he'd met Bronte in Neverwinter, married her, moved to Silverymoon for the Lady's College, and fathered Marion, the Storm Queen collected. Her claim followed the water up the **Rauvin — Silverymoon's own river** — and drowned him **at home**; no body was ever recovered. *(The Rauvin runs through Silverymoon; the Moonbridge spans it. He died on the city's river — which is why Bronte never left.)* This party's Marianne is the version from "before," who never paid that price. Bronte, alone and grieving, was recruited by Vecna's cult and placed under a geas. Her letter to Marianne was genuinely hers, but the cult turned it into bait after Olaf told Vecna that Marianne wanted to reconnect.

---

# Silverymoon (the city)

- The city still looks beautiful — the Moonbridge, the silver light — but feels hollow and quiet.
- Windows are shuttered. The Lady's College stands dark and abandoned (Marianne trained there under Alustriel).
- There is no music anywhere, which is strange for Silverymoon.
- Unstable **Crevices of Dusk** flicker at the edges of vision (a Lady of Pain ripple effect). The cult uses one during the fight.

# Key Locations

- **Bonhomme Smithy** — Marianne's father's forge. Not involved in the plot; emotional if Marianne appears. If asked carefully, he can warn that **Bronte has changed** — gone quiet, keeps odd company, goes out at night. A safe home for Marion later.
- **Bronte's Home** — the trap. An **antimagic device is hidden in the walls** (see below).
- **Lady's College** — dark, abandoned. Atmosphere only.
- **Moonbridge / Silver Chalice Tavern** — color; locals have seen Bronte walking toward the old shrine-warrens after dark.
- **The Shrine Warrens** — an old, half-abandoned undercity quarter of small shrines and prayer-cells, from when Silverymoon openly honored many faiths; shuttered and forgotten under Vecna's suppression of worship. The local **Vecna cult cell nests here** (Silverymoon's mirror of Waterdeep's Undercroft). **Bronte slips here at night** to meet her handlers and take the cult's instructions — the source of her Geas — and the **Vecna Null and assassins were staged here** before being moved into her home. *Optional lead:* a suspicious party that **tails Bronte here** instead of walking into the reunion can catch the cult mid-planning and blow the trap open early (a reward for paranoia, and an alternate way into the fight).

# The Reunion

- Bronte opens the door before they knock, overjoyed, and pulls Marianne into a warm hug. It is genuine.
- **Marion (11)** is present, watching warily. He is a coached, frightened kid — **not** under any magic. He doesn't know this stranger is his father (his father "died").
- Bronte invites them to stay for dinner. She is stalling, buying time to give the signal.

# Red Flags

Let the players earn the dread. DCs are high — this is a level-15 party.

| Clue | Check | DC |
|---|---|---|
| Bronte never asks **how Marianne is alive** | Insight | 20 |
| Marion's answers are **rehearsed** (he glances at Bronte first) | Insight | 18 |
| The air in the house feels **wrong** (the antimagic device) | Arcana | 18 |
| Bronte is under a **geas** | Detect Magic, or Arcana | 20 |
| She keeps touching a **pendant** at her throat | Passive Perception | 16 |

# What a Geas Is (plain)

- A geas is a **spell** — not an object, a creature, or a spirit.
- It's an invisible magical **command** placed on a person's mind. The caster gives an order; the target is magically forced to obey.
- Acting against the order **hurts them** (psychic damage) and can stop them mid-action. The geas can't think — it only enforces the order it was given.
- It can't be seen, only detected as magic. **Remove Curse** or **Greater Restoration** ends it.
- Here: the cult's order to Bronte is *lure Marianne in, then don't interfere with the ambush.* She physically cannot warn them.

# The Antimagic Field (dormant until the trap springs)

A device hidden in the walls can flood the house with an antimagic field — but it is **dormant until Bronte triggers the pendant.** Until then, magic works normally, so the party won't notice it just by walking in.

- **Finding it early takes looking.** *Detect Magic* senses a strong **abjuration woven into the walls**; otherwise **Arcana or Investigation DC 18** notices the room is subtly *wrong* — too cold, too quiet, the air oddly heavy. A real red flag, but only if a suspicious player actively checks.
- **When the pendant fires,** the field snaps to **full antimagic.** While the **Vecna Null** lives, **all spells (cantrips included) and magic items fail** — the equalizer for an overpowered party. *(Non-spell features still work: Rage, Sneak Attack, a paladin's Divine Smite and Lay on Hands, etc.)*
- **Flavor:** the opposite of the cold, silent phylactery vault — the instant it activates, the air goes thick, heavy, and wool-muffled.

# The Geas, the Null, and the Failsafe (the engine)

- The **Vecna Null** is a hollow cultist-construct. It **projects the antimagic field** (it is the device) and **holds the leash** on Bronte's geas.
- **While the Null lives:** the field is up, so the party can't break the geas with magic, and can't out-magic the fight.
- **The failsafe (why Bronte almost certainly dies):** the instant the leash is cut — the Null dies, *or* Bronte breaks the geas, *or* she turns on the cult — Vecna's failsafe fires and **Bronte is instantly reduced to 0 HP (no save)**, left dying; she can't be stabilized normally, and only **Greater Restoration, Regenerate, or comparable magic that same round** pulls her back. The field drops at the same moment (magic returns next turn).
- So the thing they must do to free her (kill the Null) is the thing that kills her. They can try to save her in the window after, but between the ongoing fight and Marion, she dies in almost every line of play. *(Thin escape hatch: only savable if someone has a readied Greater Restoration / mass heal in the same round the Null dies and Marion is already secured.)*

# The Trap Springs

- Bronte gets up to "check dinner" and presses her pendant — the signal.
- Enemies enter from the cellar, the bedroom, and through a **Crevice of Dusk** that splits the back wall.
- Bronte does not help and does not flee (the geas won't let her). She stands between the assassins and Marion, unable to speak.
- Roll initiative.

# Containment — Keeping Them in the House

A level-15 party can normally just teleport out of a bad scene, so containment is built in — layered, not railroaded.

- **Magic escape is dead.** While the **Vecna Null** lives, its antimagic field fills the house: **Teleport, Teleportation Circle, Dimension Door, Misty Step, Plane Shift, Word of Recall, Tree Stride, Gate** — all fail. The only way to drop the field is to kill the Null, which fires the failsafe that kills Bronte. So the field holds them for the whole meaningful part of the fight.
- **Physical escape is barred (mechanically, not magically).** On the pendant signal, the cult's pre-rigged **iron drop-bars/shutters** slam over the doors and windows — a spring mechanism, so it works *inside* the antimagic field (don't use *Arcane Lock*; it'd be suppressed). Forcing one: an **action + Athletics DC 20**, or break it (barred door/shutter = **AC 17, 30 HP, resistant to nonmagical damage**). It costs tempo, not their whole plan.
- **The real lock is motivation.** **Marion is in the room.** No one abandons a terrified 11-year-old to four assassins, and Marianne won't leave Bronte. Their own priorities pin them there better than any wall — lean on this first; the field and bars are just insurance against one "screw it, I teleport us out" player.
- **Give them an honest exit valve.** If they grab Marion and batter out through a shutter, **let them** — it doesn't save Bronte (the failsafe/cult still finishes her), so the gut-punch lands either way. Containment only needs to stop an *instant* magical bail before the scene can breathe.
- **Paranoia nuance:** the field is **dormant until the pendant**, so magic works normally on arrival — the early tell isn't a fizzle but *Detect Magic* sensing the abjuration in the walls (or Arcana/Investigation DC 18 clocking how *wrong* the room feels).

# The Fight

**Enemies:** 1 **Vecna Null** + 4 **Vecna Cult Assassins**. *(Pre-loaded — see initiative note below.)*

Difficulty layering for an overpowered party:

- **Antimagic field** (while the Null lives) — removes the party's spell advantage.
- **Marion in the room** — no AoE. The assassins fight near him on purpose, using him as a soft shield.
- **Tight quarters + rigged house** — on round 3 (or when first bloodied), the cult sets an alchemist's-fire cache. Fire spreads 1 square per round; anyone ending their turn in a burning square takes **1d6 fire.**
- **Crevice of Dusk** — if the fight runs long (round 4), one more assassin or a single **wraith** steps through.
- **Bronte in the way** — the assassins keep her between themselves and the party.

**The shape of it:** the party will rush the Null to free Bronte. The moment it dies, the failsafe fires and Bronte drops, the field falls, and they're left with a too-late window while still fighting assassins and shielding Marion.

> **Pre-loaded initiative.** On the dashboard's **⏱️ Initiative Tracker** tab, click **Load: Bronte Trap** — it drops in the Vecna Null, all four assassins, the party, plus Bronte (geas-bound) and Marion (protect), ready to roll.

# Bronte's Death

- When the Null dies, the geas dies with it and the real Bronte surfaces for a moment — then the failsafe kills her.
- Plain version: she gets one clear-headed moment, puts herself between Marion and the room, and dies.
- If the party lands the near-impossible save, let them have it. But build for the loss.
- **Optional:** if a player chooses to put Bronte down themselves — because the geas keeps her in the way, or to spare her the failsafe — let them. That's Marianne's to carry.

# Aftermath

- **Marion is the achievable win** — save the boy even though they can't save Bronte.
  - Marianne takes him (a stranger with his father's face), or
  - He goes to his grandfather at the **Bonhomme Smithy.**
  - He may blame the party, or Marianne. Don't resolve it cheaply.
- **Marianne's choice:** stay, take Marion, bury Bronte. There are no clean options.
- **DM secret (don't reveal):** Olaf caused this by telling Vecna. Play his reactions for Wren.
- **Sets up Feywild:** the Palace of Heart's Desire will later offer Marianne a vision of Bronte and Marion alive and happy — and dare him to stay.

# Rewards & Consequences

- No clean loot — the reward is story. Optional: Bronte's **pendant** (the signal-focus) is a minor cult relic; cleansed, it becomes a Power-of-Secrets hook pointing to the cult's Silverymoon handler.
- **Marion** as a recurring NPC.
- **Level up at end** (or hold it — your call).

# Stat Blocks

**Vecna Null**
*Medium undead (construct-touched), neutral evil — a hollow-eyed husk in a cultist's robes; the air around it is dead and muffled.*
**AC** 16 · **HP** 150 (20d8+60) · **Speed** 20 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 15 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 10 (+0) |

**Saves** Con +7, Wis +7 · **Resistances** necrotic; nonmagical B/P/S · **Condition Immunities** charmed, frightened, paralyzed · **Senses** blindsight 60 ft. · **CR** 12

- **Null Field.** A 30-ft.-radius **antimagic field** (as the spell) surrounds the Null and fills the house. Spells and magic-item activations inside it fail. The Null is unaffected by its own field.
- **Geas Warden.** While the Null lives, the geas on Bronte **cannot be removed.**
- **Severance (failsafe).** When the Null drops to 0 HP — or if Bronte breaks the geas or turns on the cult — **Bronte is instantly reduced to 0 HP, no save** (left dying; only Greater Restoration / Regenerate-tier magic that same round saves her). The Null's field collapses at the same instant (magic returns next initiative count).

**Actions**
- **Multiattack.** Two *Nullifying Touch* attacks.
- **Nullifying Touch.** *Melee, +6 to hit, reach 5 ft.* — 13 (3d6+2) necrotic, and the target can't regain hit points until the end of its next turn.
- **Hollow Word (recharge 5–6).** A soundless pulse. Each non-undead creature within 15 ft. makes a **DC 15 Wisdom save** or is *stunned* until the end of its next turn.

**Vecna Cult Assassin** *(×4)*
*Medium humanoid, neutral evil — robed killers with eye-sigil brands.*
**AC** 16 (studded leather) · **HP** 78 (12d8+24) · **Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11 (+0) | 18 (+4) | 14 (+2) | 13 (+1) | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) |

**Saves** Dex +7, Int +4 · **Skills** Stealth +7 · **CR** 8

- **Assassinate.** Advantage on attacks vs. any creature that hasn't taken a turn; a hit on a surprised creature is a critical.
- **Sneak Attack (1/turn).** +14 (4d6) when it has advantage or an ally is within 5 ft. of the target.
- **Cruel Shield.** When within 5 ft. of **Marion or Bronte**, attacks that miss the assassin by 5+ risk the bystander (DM's discretion).

**Actions**
- **Multiattack.** Two *Shortsword* or *Hand Crossbow* attacks.
- **Shortsword.** *+7 to hit, reach 5 ft.* — 7 (1d6+4) piercing + 3 (1d6) necrotic.
- **Hand Crossbow.** *+7 to hit, range 30/120 ft.* — 7 (1d6+4) piercing, plus Sneak Attack if eligible.

**Bronte Bonhomme — Grief-Bound (Cult-Compelled)**
*Medium humanoid (human), neutral (under compulsion) — Marianne's wife, a former agent hollowed by grief and bound by Vecna's Geas.*
**AC** 15 (studded leather) · **HP** 110 (17d8+34) · **Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11 (+0) | 16 (+3) | 15 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 14 (+2) |

**Saves** Dex +6, Wis +3 · **Skills** Deception +5, Insight +3, Perception +3, Stealth +6 · **CR** 7 · **Ability save DC 15**

- **Geas-Bound.** While the Vecna Null lives, she can't willingly flee or aid the party — she acts as the cult commands. Break the Geas (*Remove Curse / Greater Restoration*) and she chooses her own actions for as long as she lives.
- **The Failsafe.** When the Null drops to 0 HP — or she breaks the Geas / turns on the cult — **Bronte is instantly reduced to 0 HP, no save**, and left dying; she can't be stabilized normally, and only **Greater Restoration, Regenerate, or comparable magic that same round** saves her.

**Actions**
- **Multiattack.** Two Rapier attacks, or one save-forcing ability and one attack.
- **Rapier.** *+6 to hit, reach 5 ft.* — 7 (1d8+3) piercing + 7 (2d6) necrotic.
- **Hand Crossbow.** *+6 to hit, 30/120 ft.* — 6 (1d6+3) piercing.
- **Wail of What Was Lost (recharge 5–6).** Each creature of her choice within 30 ft. that can hear her makes a **DC 15 Wisdom save** — fail: 22 (5d8) psychic and **frightened of Bronte** until the end of its next turn; success: half, not frightened. **Marianne has disadvantage.**
- **Binding Grip (recharge 6).** One creature within 5 ft. makes a **DC 15 Strength save** or is **grappled and restrained** (escape DC 15) while she clings on — the assassins have **advantage** vs. the restrained target.

**Reaction**
- **Cult's Compulsion.** When a creature moves to reach **Marion or the Null**, Bronte moves up to her speed to interpose.

# Quick Reference

1. **Silverymoon** — beautiful but hollow; no music; Crevices of Dusk.
2. **Reunion at Bronte's home** — warm welcome, Marion present, dinner stall.
3. **Red flags** — high DCs (Insight 20/18, Arcana 18/20, Passive Perception 16).
4. **Antimagic field** — warm and smothering (not the vault's cold); Arcana DC 18 to notice; full strength once the trap springs.
