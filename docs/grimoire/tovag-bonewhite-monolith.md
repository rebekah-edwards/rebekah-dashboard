# Chapter: Tovag — The Bonewhite Monolith

*Second obelisk attunement. Kas's abandoned Domain of Dread — oppressive **survival horror**, with the necrotic mists draining the party, a couple of set-piece fights, and a single **guardian** to defeat at the obelisk. Kas himself is **gone**, but his return looms over everything. Plain descriptions; color at the table.*

## Running This Chapter

The party reaches Tovag using **Jimothy's mist talisman (the Talisman of Bleeding Veils)** — they walk the mists into the domain *(alternatively, a Cyre 1313 Planar Resonance Window)*. **Tovag** is **Kas's** Domain of Dread, set near the **Negative Energy Plane**, where the betrayer-lieutenant was exiled as a **vampire** and warred endlessly against Vecna's adjacent domain, **Cavitius**. The party comes to attune the second of the five Netherese obelisks, the **Bonewhite Monolith.**

**Kas is not here.** He has discovered what the obelisks do and has gone to seize them himself — which is the chapter's central revelation (see Power of Secrets). His empty domain is leaderless, restless, and dangerous, and the dread that he might *return* hangs over the whole chapter.

## Character Advancement

The characters are 15th level. They **gain a level** after attuning the obelisk (**area T6**).

## Power of Secrets

One secret here, and it doubles as the **concrete Sword-of-Kas lead**:

> 🔒 **Kas's Gambit** — from **Corvath the Forsworn** (area **T5**) and confirmed by Kas's own war-records (area **T4**). Kas has learned the obelisks can be attuned, and he means to **seize them himself to overthrow Vecna.** He has taken the **Sword of Kas** and gone to the **last obelisk, at Karsus' Enclave (Netheril).** *That* is where the party will face him — and where the Sword can finally be claimed. Learning this counts as a secret.

## The Obelisk

The **Bonewhite Monolith** (2nd of 5): bonewhite stone, **warm to the touch**, ringed by scorched runes that **smolder but never burn**, standing in a stone circle wreathed in gray mist and rising red embers, two volcanic peaks behind it under a blood-red sky. **Zephyr's hook:** his **Dolindar** lineage stirs strongest here — fuzzy visions of his ancestors' **failed** struggle to control the Netherese obelisks sharpen into something useful (see T6).

> ### Domain Features (survival horror — apply throughout)
> - **The Draining Mists.** While in Tovag, a creature **can't regain hit points.** At the end of each area (or each hour), each creature makes a **DC 15 Constitution save**; on a failure, its **hit point maximum drops by 1d6** until it leaves the domain and finishes a long rest. This is the equalizer — the party can't heal their way through.
> - **A dead, freezing cold** (Negative Energy Plane bleed): a creature that rolls a 1 on the mist save also gains a level of **exhaustion.**
> - **Ash storms & lava fissures** across the battlefield (difficult terrain, occasional fire damage).
> - **The endless war.** The battlefield dead — Kas's vampiric host and Vecna's undead legions — can **reignite their war** around the party at any time (see T2).

# T1. The Ashen Threshold

> *The mists part and spit you out onto black volcanic rock under a sky the color of a deep bruise. Two jagged peaks smolder on the horizon. Ash falls like grey snow, and everything — the air, the ground, your own breath — is cold in a way that feels wrong, like the cold of a thing that has been dead a long time. Far off, on a crag, a black citadel waits. And between you and it lies a battlefield that goes on forever.*

**Contents.** The arrival point. Establish the **Draining Mists** here (let them feel the no-healing rule immediately) and the oppressive dread. No combat yet — let the place do the work.

- **Zephyr** feels his pendant grow warm and heavy the moment he arrives; the obelisk is near, and so is his family's old failure.
- The only way forward is **across the battlefield (T2)** to the **citadel (T3)**; the obelisk stands beyond the citadel (T6).

---

# T2. The Battlefield of the Endless War

> *The dead are everywhere — vampiric soldiers and Vecna's undead, locked together where they fell, frozen mid-killing-blow across miles of ash. As you pick your way through, the silence breaks: somewhere, a war-horn sounds, and the corpses begin, slowly, to stir.*

**Contents — Set-Piece #1.** Crossing the battlefield reignites the **endless war.** The dead rise and resume their fight — and the party is caught in the middle. Run this as a **moving skirmish**, not a static brawl: the party fights *through* a battle that doesn't care about them, using the chaos (and the lava fissures) as cover and weapon.

- **Suggested foes** (scale to taste): a wave of **wights** and **vampire spawn** (Kas's side) clashing with **wraiths** and **ghouls** (Vecna's side). The party can let the two sides grind each other down, or get swarmed if they linger.
- **Hazards:** ash storms (lightly obscured), lava fissures (3d6 fire if knocked in), and the **mist save** at the end.
- **Out:** reaching the citadel gate ends the skirmish — the risen dead won't follow onto Kas's ground.

---

# T3. The Black Citadel — Hollow Halls

> *Kas's fortress is black basalt and dead silence. No guards. No torches. Your footsteps echo too long. This was the seat of a warlord who has ruled here for an age — and it is utterly, recently empty, as though he stepped out moments ago and simply never came back.*

**Contents.** Atmosphere and dread. The citadel is abandoned; the horror is the **emptiness** and the sense of a monster who could return at any second. Things to find:

- **The empty throne**, and above it, an **empty sword-rack** — the shape of a great blade still pressed into the dust where it hung for centuries. *(The Sword of Kas is not here. He took it. Foreshadow.)*
- War-trophies, banners of two armies, and corridors that all seem to lead deeper in.
- A sense, growing, that they are **not supposed to be here** — and that something will be very angry that they were.

# T4. The War Room

> *A great table dominates this chamber, buried under maps, muster-rolls, and battle-plans in a sharp, ancient hand. Most of it is the endless war with Cavitius. But the newest marks aren't about Cavitius at all.*

**Contents — the lead.** Kas's most recent records lay his plan bare (an **Investigation DC 15**, or just reading the freshest maps):

- Kas has marked the **five obelisks** across the planes — and crossed several off.
- His campaign has turned **away** from his war with Vecna and **toward seizing the obelisks himself.**
- The final marks point to **Karsus' Enclave (Netheril)** — and a note that he rides there now, **Sword in hand.**

This is the documentary half of **Secret #1 (Kas's Gambit)**; Corvath (T5) gives it a voice and the rest of the truth.

**Treasure.** A *war-banner of Tovag* (worth a small fortune to a collector), and a sealed case holding **2 vials of a black, cold liquid** — *Potions of Vitality* twisted by the domain (act as Greater Healing **only outside** the mists; inside Tovag they do nothing — a grim joke).

---

# T5. Corvath the Forsworn (the trapped ally)

> *A figure kneels in the dark of a cell that has no lock — a gaunt, translucent knight in the shattered tabard of Kas's herald, his banner broken across his knees. He does not rise. "You're alive," he says, and the word sounds like envy and pity at once. "You shouldn't be here. He'll feel it. He feels everything that touches his ground."*

**Contents.** **Corvath the Forsworn** — once **Kas's own herald**, the man who carried the vampire's words into battle. Generations ago he **broke his oath**: ordered to deliver the command for a massacre, he refused, and tried to end the endless war. Kas's punishment was worse than death — Corvath is **bound to Tovag, unable to die or leave**, condemned to watch the war repeat forever. He is lucid, grieving, and hollowed out by time. He is not hostile; he is **tired.**

**What Corvath gives:**

- **Lore** on Tovag, Kas, and the war — and the truth of why the citadel is empty.
- 🔒 **Secret #1 — Kas's Gambit** (voiced; pairs with the War Room): Kas has gone to take the obelisks for himself, Sword in hand, to **become** what Vecna is. He's at **Karsus' Enclave.** Corvath knows because heralds carry their lord's intent. *"He doesn't want to kill the Whispering One anymore. He wants to replace him."*
- A **warning** about the obelisk's guardian (T6) — the **Cairn Warden** — and how it feeds on the battlefield dead.
- If the party treats him with mercy, he offers what little help he can: he can **walk them safely to the obelisk** (no mist save on the way), or **hold the risen dead at bay** while they attune (removing the "waves" pressure from T6).

> **The mercy beat.** Corvath's anchor is his **broken herald's horn**, which keeps him bound. The party can **shatter it** to finally release him. If they do, he gives them his thanks and his blessing — *the Forsworn's Boon:* once before this chapter ends, a character may **reroll a failed save against fear, necrotic, or exhaustion.* Then he simply... lets go, and is gone. A kind death, the first kindness Tovag has seen in an age.

# T6. The Bonewhite Monolith

> *Beyond the citadel, in a circle of standing stones, the obelisk waits: bonewhite, warm even at this distance, its scorched runes smoldering without ever catching. Gray mist coils around it; red embers drift upward into the bruised sky. And rising from the bone-litter at its base, something vast unfolds itself to meet you.*

**Contents — the guardian, then the attunement.**

> ### The Cairn Warden (the guardian)
> *Huge undead, neutral evil — a colossus of fused battlefield bone and scorched armor, raised by Kas to keep the obelisk for himself.*
> **AC** 18 (natural) · **HP** 264 (23d12+115) · **Speed** 40 ft.
> **STR** 24 (+7) · **DEX** 8 (−1) · **CON** 21 (+5) · **INT** 6 (−2) · **WIS** 14 (+2) · **CHA** 12 (+1)
> **Saving Throws** Con +11, Wis +8 · **Damage Resistances** cold, fire; nonmagical B/P/S · **Damage Immunities** necrotic, poison · **Condition Immunities** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, poisoned · **Senses** blindsight 90 ft. · **CR** 17
> **Hoarder of the Fallen.** The Warden has advantage on attacks while at least one allied undead is within 30 ft. of it.
> **Born of the Battlefield.** The Warden can't regain HP, but the surrounding battlefield can't run out of dead, either (see Call the Fallen).
> **Actions.** *Multiattack.* Two **Slam** attacks: +13 to hit, reach 15 ft., 22 (3d10+7) bludgeoning plus 9 (2d8) necrotic, and the target can't regain HP until the end of its next turn.
> *Call the Fallen (recharge 5–6).* The Warden drags the battlefield dead up around the obelisk: **1d4+1 wights** (or vampire spawn) rise within 30 ft. and act on the Warden's initiative. *(If Corvath is holding the dead at bay, this fails — reward the mercy.)*
> *Grave Wind (1/day).* A 60-ft. cone of grave-cold: **DC 17 Con save**, 45 (10d8) cold on a fail (half on success), and failed targets have their **hit point maximum reduced** by the damage until they leave Tovag.
>
> **Difficulty note:** with the no-healing mists and Call the Fallen, this single guardian *is* the whole fight. If the party is mopping it up too easily, lean on Call the Fallen and a timely ash storm. If they freed/befriended Corvath, let his help genuinely lighten it — that's the payoff.

**Attuning the obelisk.** With the Warden down, the party performs the attunement (the Rod and their Vecna's Link resonate; a roughly hour-long trance, as at Ythryn). **No card draw this time** *(consistent with recent sessions — add one if you want)*.

> **Zephyr's vision (Dolindar).** As he attunes, Zephyr sees his ancestors **try this and fail** — and sees *why*: the Netherese who came before reached for the obelisks' power to **control** it, and were unmade. The lesson lands: the obelisks must be **restored**, not seized. He also glimpses, at the edge of the vision, **Kas reaching for that same power with the Sword** — sharpening the lead toward Karsus' Enclave.

> ### The Almost-Kas Scare (climax)
> The instant the attunement completes, the domain **reacts**: the mist parts in a long corridor toward the horizon, a sound like a great blade being drawn hums up through the stone, and every dead thing in Tovag turns its head the same direction at once. **Kas felt that. He knows.** Nothing comes — but the party should leave *now*, certain that the next time they feel that presence, he'll be standing in front of them. *(Pure dread; no Kas. This sets up his confusion-sowing return at the finale.)*

**Rewards.**
- **Second obelisk attuned** (Bonewhite Monolith). Obelisks **2/5**.
- 🔒 **Secret #1 — Kas's Gambit** (the Sword-of-Kas lead toward Karsus' Enclave).
- **Zephyr's lore** deepened; the Dolindar thread and the Sword's role come into focus.
- *(If earned:)* the **Forsworn's Boon**, and the quiet good of having freed Corvath.
- **LEVEL UP.**

# Appendix — Quick Sequence

**T1 Ashen Threshold** *(mist rules)* → **T2 Battlefield** *(skirmish #1)* → **T3 Hollow Halls** *(empty throne + empty sword-rack)* → **T4 War Room** *(the documentary lead)* → **T5 Corvath** *(Secret #1 + mercy + help)* → **T6 Bonewhite Monolith** *(Cairn Warden → attune → Zephyr's vision → almost-Kas scare → level up)*.

**Survival-horror spine:** the mists mean no healing and a shrinking HP max, so every fight and every hour costs them. Freeing Corvath is the one mercy that makes the climax easier — reward it.
