📝 Key Decisions Still Needed

  • Printing costs: Need final quotes to validate tier pricing and margins
  • Campaign video: Who's producing? Budget? Timeline?
  • Final cover designs: When will these be locked?
  • Audiobook production: Narrator selection, studio costs — needed for stretch goal budgeting
  • TTRPG scope: How much content is ready? What's the production cost to finish?
  • Investor/loan: Additional $20-30K would significantly increase ad spend and campaign potential
  • Influencer budget: Free copies + affiliate commission vs. paid sponsorships

🎯 Positioning Reminder

  • Lead with: "Storytelling-first fiction" · "Books that prioritize great stories" · "Publisher built by readers, for readers"
  • Avoid leading with: Anti-woke framing, political positioning, what you're against
  • Let the community discover: The "based" angle comes through naturally in podcast conversations, X threads, and community engagement — not in ad copy or the BackerKit page
