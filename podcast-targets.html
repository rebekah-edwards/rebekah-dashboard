📝 Pitch Templates

For entertainment commentary channels:

"We're launching a new fiction publisher that puts story first — no ideology tests, no sensitivity readers gatekeeping great fiction. We're going direct to readers via BackerKit because we believe the best stories shouldn't need permission from New York publishers."

For BookTube/fantasy reviewers:

"Lion Publishing is a new storytelling-first publisher launching our first titles via BackerKit in October 2026. We'd love to share our vision for what a modern publisher can look like and how we're building something that serves both authors and readers."

For indie publishing podcasts:

"We're building a publisher specifically for genre fiction authors who want creative freedom and a direct relationship with their readers. Our BackerKit model means authors keep more, readers get involved earlier, and the stories stay true to the author's vision."

For adaptation podcasts:

"We host The Book Is Better — a podcast reviewing book-to-film adaptations, about 60 episodes in. We'd love to do a guest swap or cross-promotion. Our audiences overlap perfectly."
